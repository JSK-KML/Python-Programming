# Tutorial 12 : Chapter 7 - Counter-Controlled Loops (Sequence Generation)

### Exercise A: Library Fine Calculator <Badge type="tip" text="Question" />

A library processes **5 books** today. The book IDs are `401, 406, 411, 416, 421` (each 5 more than the last), and the days overdue are `3, 6, 9, 12, 15` (each 3 more than the last). The fine is RM0.50 per overdue day, but no book is fined more than RM8.00. Using a `for` loop that generates the book IDs and overdue days from the loop counter, print each book's ID, days overdue, and fine.

### Exercise B: Taxi Fare System <Badge type="tip" text="Question" />

A taxi company records **5 trips**. The trip numbers are `205, 210, 215, 220, 225` (each 5 more than the last), and the distances are `5, 8, 11, 14, 17` km (each 3 more than the last). The fare is a base of RM4.00 plus RM1.20 per km, with an extra RM2.00 surcharge when the distance is more than 10 km. Using a `for` loop that generates the trip numbers and distances from the loop counter, print each trip's number, distance, and total fare.

### Exercise C: Employee Bonus Calculator <Badge type="tip" text="Question" />

HR calculates bonuses for **5 employees**. The employee IDs are `1002, 1007, 1012, 1017, 1022` (each 5 more than the last), and the years of service are `2, 4, 6, 8, 10` (each 2 more than the last). The bonus is a base of RM300 plus RM50 per year of service, with an extra loyalty bonus of RM150 when service is 6 years or more. Using a `for` loop that generates the IDs and years from the loop counter, print each employee's ID, years of service, and total bonus.

### Exercise D: Internet Data Plan <Badge type="tip" text="Question" />

An ISP bills **5 customers**. The account numbers are `5001, 5004, 5007, 5010, 5013` (each 3 more than the last), and the data used is `15, 25, 35, 45, 55` GB (each 10 more than the last). The monthly fee is RM40, plus RM3 for every GB used above a 20 GB limit, with an extra RM15 high-usage fee when usage is 40 GB or more. Using a `for` loop that generates the account numbers and data used from the loop counter, print each account's number, data used, and total bill.

### Exercise E: Parking Meter System <Badge type="tip" text="Question" />

A city calculates parking fees for **5 zones**. The zone codes are `302, 307, 312, 317, 322` (each 5 more than the last), and the hours parked are `1, 3, 5, 7, 9` (each 2 more than the last). The rate is RM2.00 per hour, but the first hour is free, and no zone is charged more than RM12.00 in a day. Using a `for` loop that generates the zone codes and hours from the loop counter, print each zone's code, hours parked, billable hours, and fee.
